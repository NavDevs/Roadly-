FAQ

Things people ask before they download.

Is Roadly free?

Yes — completely. There are no in-app purchases and no premium tier. Download it, report hazards, earn points, done.

What do I need to install it?

Any phone running Android 5.0 or newer. The app is a single ~54 MB APK file. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time — that's a one-tap permission.

Do I need an account?

Just your phone number, verified once with an SMS code. That's what ties your points and report history to you — no passwords, no email forms.

How long do my reports stay on the map?

Until they're no longer true. Each hazard type has its own expiry — fires clear in about an hour, accidents in two, roadworks in four, potholes can stay up to 48 hours. The map cleans itself, so you never drive toward a stale pin.

Will it use up my data?

Barely. A report is one compressed photo plus your coordinates — a few hundred kilobytes at most. The map only streams what's near you.

What happens to my location?

Location is read only when you submit a report, and it's used to place that hazard on the map for everyone else. Your own location isn't shared with other users.
